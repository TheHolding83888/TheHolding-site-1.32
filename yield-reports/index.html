Our Approach

Slow money.
Compound time.

The Holding is built around dedicated income engines — funds designed to generate recurring cash flow, reported separately from asset-price performance. Defitea works through vote-escrowed DeFi positions and liquidity provision. Monetra through stablecoin strategies. Fructus through tokenised real-world asset yield. Together they form a multi-layered cash-flow system. The strategies remain exposed to market, protocol, liquidity, and asset-specific risks.

These reports capture that income. Not paper gains. Not price appreciation. Actual dollars produced monthly — the goal being a system that compounds reliably across years, through bull markets, bear markets, and everything in between.

Income Funds

Three yield mandates.

Each income-generating fund within The Holding operates a distinct cash-flow strategy. Different risk profiles, different yield sources, unified purpose.

Defitea
Cash Flow Engine

The primary income fund of The Holding. Capital deployed into DeFi's most proven yield mechanisms: vote-escrowed governance tokens, liquidity provision, and protocol revenue sharing. Positions are committed long-term — accepting reduced liquidity in exchange for durable cash flow. A growing set of productive DeFi positions, each contributing to a blended monthly cash flow that varies with market and protocol conditions.

Stable Yield Layer

The liquidity engine of The Holding. Capital deployed exclusively into stablecoin strategies — lending markets, stable liquidity pools, staked stablecoins, and automated agent-managed yield. Jan–Jul 2026 preserve the original reported Defitea cash-flow history. Automated periods are generated by The Holding Reporting Layer from daily productive TVL snapshots and the latest validated Reference APR. Those automated values are a reference cash-flow model, not claim accounting, and the current month remains provisional until the first daily snapshot of the next month closes it. Monthly yield is cash flow divided by average capital; closed-month annualised APR is the average monthly yield multiplied by twelve, with no compounding. Asset-price performance remains separate. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
