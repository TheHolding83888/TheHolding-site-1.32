Etymology

The language behind the architecture. Five names, five functions — each chosen to express the principle of the fund it represents.

Substantia

substantia
Latin: substance · essence · underlying reality

Substantia refers to that of which something consists — its substance, essence, and underlying reality. The name carries the idea of what stands beneath.

That is the role of Substantia within The Holding: the foundation layer on which the rest of the architecture is built. Bitcoin, Ethereum, gold, and silver form its long-term reserve base.

Substance over speculation.
Foundation before expansion.

Defitea

DeFi + Tea
The origin of a productive-capital layer

The name began with a single cup of premium green tea. One of the first harvests from an early DeFi strategy generated enough cash flow to buy something tangible — not to reinvest, not to compound, but simply to enjoy. That small purchase made the idea real.

Onchain positions could do more than rise or fall in price. They could perform economic work and produce usable cash flow. Defitea became the name of The Holding's productive capital layer.

Cash flow made tangible.
One harvest at a time.

Monetra

monēta
Inspired by Latin monēta: mint · coin · money

In ancient Rome, coinage was closely associated with the Temple of Juno Moneta on the Capitoline Hill. From monēta came a monetary lineage that still lives in words such as money, mint, and monetary.

Monetra is a modern adaptation of that ancient root. The name belongs to the liquidity layer of The Holding — the part of the architecture designed to keep capital available, flexible, and ready to support the wider system.

An ancient monetary root.
A modern liquidity mandate.

Fructus

frūctus
Latin: fruit · produce · proceeds · income

In Latin, frūctus described both the fruit of something and the benefit produced by it. In Roman law, it could refer to the economic fruits of property — crops from land, rent from buildings, and other proceeds generated by a productive asset.

The distinction is fundamental: not the sale of the asset, but what the asset produces. That is the mandate of Fructus — connecting The Holding to tokenised real-world assets and the economic activity behind them.

Not the sale of the tree.
The fruit it produces.

Singul

singularity
A point beyond which previous assumptions no longer apply

A singularity is a point beyond which previous assumptions no longer describe what comes next. In technology, it represents a transition so significant that its consequences become difficult to predict.

Singul names the smallest and most asymmetric layer of The Holding. Its purpose is to hold carefully sized exposure to frontier technologies and protocols capable of crossing from experiment to infrastructure. Not every position will succeed.

The purpose is to be present
when one changes the curve.

Five Names. One Architecture.

A name should do more than identify a fund. It should explain why the fund exists.
