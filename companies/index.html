From Companies to Infrastructure

A network that compounds more than capital.

Each company remains sovereign — its own owner, treasury, keys, strategy and history. What connects them is not custody, but a common architecture for measurement.

The Holding reads what can be verified onchain, normalizes it into a common company model, and preserves the resulting operating record over time.

Every new company adds another balance sheet, another cash-flow history and another set of protocol relationships to the shared data layer. Over time, the registry becomes a dataset; the dataset becomes intelligence; and that intelligence can support indices, ratings, discovery and eventually external data products.

The companies remain sovereign. The intelligence becomes shared infrastructure.

01 Reference APY

A current normalized measure of productive capacity. It tells what the strategy book is producing now, not what the company has already earned historically.

02 Embedded Yield

Yield that stays inside aToken, ERC-4626, PPS or other share value. It becomes historical income only after verified, flow-adjusted checkpoints can be compared.

03 Claimable Rewards

Income that has accrued protocol-side but remains separately claimable. Once received by the company wallet it becomes realised cash flow / treasury, not accrued rewards.

04 Stable Price Effect

Depeg or market-price movement is kept outside strategy income, so a change in the dollar price of a stable asset cannot masquerade as yield.

The Holding Intelligence · Live

The network does not just hold capital. It measures itself.

A dedicated data layer reads blockchains, protocol contracts and official distribution data, normalizes them into a common company model, and turns raw onchain activity into usable operating intelligence.

Blockchains→ Protocols→ Positions→ Productivity→ Rewards→ History→ Index
01 · DATA

Direct Onchain Reads

Wallets, veNFTs, locks, voting state and reward contracts are read directly wherever the result can be reproduced. External APIs remain useful inputs — not unquestioned answers.

02 · PRODUCTIVITY

Productivity Intelligence

Different productive engines are normalized into a common Reference APR framework, allowing companies to be compared without pretending their underlying mechanics are identical.

03 · REWARDS

Company Rewards

Protocol-side accrued rewards are separated from already claimed value, resolved to their payout assets and priced without double counting whenever the route can be verified.

04 · MEMORY

Operating Memory

Validated snapshots become history. That history feeds Company Passports, reporting, The Holding Index and, over time, deeper measures of resilience, cash-flow quality and reputation.

If a value cannot be reproduced and verified, The Holding does not invent it.

Today this intelligence layer serves The Holding itself. Over time, the same structured data can become a source for company owners, applications, analysts and AI agents.

How a company is born

01
Identity
A unique ENS name is registered — often based on a date of birth or personal meaning. This becomes the company's address.
02
Assets
Assets are selected and placed on the balance sheet of the company being created, based on the chosen strategy.
03
Key
Ownership is delivered through a private access setup — from a recovery phrase to a Tangem card or ring, based on the owner's preference.
04
Autonomy
The company begins to operate: productive positions accrue cash flow while its onchain activity starts building a measurable operating history.
