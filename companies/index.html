The Next Layer

A network of sovereign onchain companies.

Today, this is a growing collection of independently owned onchain companies. Over time, it will grow into a decentralized network — while each company remains sovereign, with its own owner, treasury, keys, strategy, and history.

The Holding never takes custody of their capital. It provides the architecture that connects them.

As the network grows, each company will build its own history and reputation. Together, they will form a shared onchain registry — and, over time, a new generation of indices that reflect the growth of the network.

One holding. Many sovereign companies. Each with its own treasury and keys.

How a company is born

01
Identity
A unique ENS name is registered — often based on a date of birth or personal meaning. This becomes the company's address.
02
Assets
Assets are selected and placed on the balance sheet of the company being created, based on the chosen strategy.
03
Key
Ownership is delivered through a private access setup — from a recovery phrase to a Tangem card or ring, based on the owner's preference.
04
Autonomy
The company begins to work: cash flow starts accruing through the protocols, visible onchain at any time.
