How the Engine Works

Defitea builds long-term positions across carefully selected DeFi protocols.

Each position uses the mechanism best suited to its structure — from locking and staking to delegation and liquidity provision.

The goal is simple: generate recurring cash flow while retaining the long-term growth potential of the underlying assets.

Different mechanisms.
One productive mandate.

The Protocol Layer

Defitea is built across a growing set of DeFi protocols, each performing a different function within the onchain economy.

Aerodrome and Velodrome provide liquidity and trading infrastructure across Base and Optimism.

Curve and Convex form a major layer of stablecoin liquidity, governance, and incentive markets on Ethereum.

Pendle creates markets for fixed and variable yield.

Frax, Liquity, and Resupply contribute stablecoin, credit, and lending infrastructure.

f(x) Protocol and Yield Basis provide specialised structures for capital efficiency and risk transformation.

Venice adds exposure to decentralised AI infrastructure.

Different protocols. Different economic mechanisms.
One productive system.

Commitment Architecture

Each position uses a protocol-specific mechanism — from multi-year vote-escrow locks to staking, delegation, and liquidity provision. The mechanism is chosen to match each protocol's structure, not forced into a single template.

These positions are not shares in legal companies. They are onchain economic positions defined by smart contracts and governance rules.

Cash-Flow Sources

Protocol Fees
Cash flow generated directly through trading, borrowing, settlement, and other protocol usage.
Voter Incentives
Payments from participants seeking governance-directed liquidity and emissions.
Token Emissions
Protocol-issued rewards that may decline over time or dilute the token supply.
Staking & Liquidity Rewards
Distributions generated through staking, locking, liquidity provision, and other protocol-native mechanisms.

Cash flow is recurring, but variable.
Its quality matters as much as its size.

Different protocols distribute rewards on different schedules. Defitea brings them together through one monthly harvest and reporting cycle.

Productive Capital, Not a Passive Token Basket

Most crypto portfolios hold tokens and wait for prices to rise. Defitea takes a different approach.

Each position is selected for the economic work it performs.

The assets can rise or fall in value. Their cash flow can also increase, decline, or disappear.

Defitea does not simply hold DeFi assets.
It puts capital to work.

Two Engines of Return

Two engines. One productive mandate.

Engine 01
Cash Flow
Protocol fees, voter incentives, staking rewards, token emissions, and other protocol-native distributions.
Engine 02
Asset Growth
Long-term exposure to productive DeFi assets whose value may grow as their protocols, usage, and economic importance expand — or decline if they do not.

Neither engine is guaranteed. Both are measured separately.

Defitea is not market-neutral.
Its assets remain exposed to market prices and changing protocol conditions.

Productive Does Not Mean Risk-Free

Defitea remains exposed to market, smart-contract, governance, liquidity, and protocol-specific risks.

Long-term locks can limit liquidity. Cash flow may decline or disappear. Asset values may fall.

Defitea is designed for long-term productive ownership — not to guarantee income or capital preservation.

Defitea remains exposed to:

  • market volatility
  • smart-contract vulnerabilities
  • governance changes
  • declining emissions and incentives
  • lock-duration and liquidity risk
  • protocol-specific dependencies
  • stablecoin and oracle risk
  • Layer 2 and bridge risk
  • embedded leverage
  • token dilution
  • regulatory change
  • the possibility of partial or total capital loss

Long-duration locks can prevent positions from being exited quickly. Cash flow may decline or disappear. Token prices may fall faster than distributions accumulate. Some underlying protocols use embedded leverage even when Defitea does not borrow directly at the fund level.
