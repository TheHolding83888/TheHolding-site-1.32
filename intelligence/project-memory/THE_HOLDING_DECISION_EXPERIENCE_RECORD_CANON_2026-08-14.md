# THE HOLDING — DECISION EXPERIENCE RECORD CANON
## 2026-08-14

## Purpose

The Holding should learn from real operating experience without converting hindsight, luck, persuasive storytelling, or repeated conversation into false confidence.

The system already has the correct core machinery: Grounded Brain cases, append-only owner Decision Memory, outcome observation, confidence calibration, Project Memory, and future Founder Decision DNA. This canon does **not** create another runtime layer. It defines the minimum evidence quality required for a real decision → outcome learning cycle.

## Core principle

**Experience must be captured before the outcome is known whenever the decision is meant to teach the system.**

A useful experience record is:

`proposal / choice → information available at decision time → owner decision → rationale → confidence → expected result → evaluation criterion → review horizon → counterevidence / invalidation condition → later observed outcome → decision-quality review → outcome-quality review → lesson`

If the pre-outcome record does not exist, later reconstruction may be kept as historical context, but it must not be treated as equally strong evidence for calibration or Founder Decision DNA.

## Minimum pre-outcome record

For material decisions that are intended to become learning evidence, capture:

1. **Decision / disposition** — accept, defer, reject, or modify.
2. **Rationale** — why the decision was made using information available at that time.
3. **Confidence** — the confidence held before the outcome, preferably probabilistic when the case is calibratable.
4. **Expected outcome** — what the decision is expected to produce.
5. **Evaluation criterion** — what observable evidence would count as success, failure, or unresolved.
6. **Review horizon** — when the outcome should be evaluated.
7. **Counterevidence** — the strongest relevant fact or argument against the chosen direction.
8. **Invalidation condition** — what would make the recommendation/decision materially wrong or require reversal.
9. **Rejected/deferred alternative** — when material, what attractive alternative was not chosen and why.

The existing Decision Ledger remains the canonical append-only owner-decision store. This canon defines evidence quality; it does not authorize duplicate ledgers.

## Decision quality is not outcome quality

The Holding must evaluate these separately.

### Decision quality

Ask: **Was the reasoning good given the information actually available at decision time?**

Signals include:
- evidence quality and completeness;
- whether material uncertainty was represented honestly;
- whether alternatives were considered;
- whether the downside / counterevidence was understood;
- whether the chosen action respected reversibility, authority, and risk constraints;
- whether confidence was calibrated to evidence rather than conviction.

A good decision can have a bad outcome because of randomness, market movement, third-party failure, or information that did not yet exist.

### Outcome quality

Ask: **What actually happened relative to the pre-recorded expectation and evaluation criterion?**

Outcome quality measures reality. It must not retroactively rewrite what was known at decision time.

### Learning rule

- Good decision + good outcome → reinforce cautiously.
- Good decision + bad outcome → study variance / external causes before changing the decision rule.
- Bad decision + good outcome → **do not reward luck**; preserve the reasoning defect.
- Bad decision + bad outcome → strongest negative learning evidence when causality is reasonably supported.

Confidence must not rise merely because an outcome was favorable.

## Founder Decision DNA — learn strongly from refusals

Reject, defer, and modify decisions are first-class learning evidence, not failed or secondary records.

They often reveal the founder's actual operating preferences more precisely than abstract declarations, especially when the rejected option was attractive.

Examples of high-value refusal evidence:
- declining unnecessary architecture despite potential elegance;
- preferring reconstruction/on-demand evidence over an always-on collector when history is reproducible;
- refusing production authority expansion before proof;
- choosing a bounded reversible fix over a broad rewrite;
- rejecting code that exists only to make the architecture look richer.

Founder Decision DNA should therefore weight repeated evidence from **choices under trade-off**, including what was deliberately not built or not authorized.

## Anti-sycophancy / mandatory challenge principle

The Holding AI must not become a high-memory agreement machine.

For material proposals, reasoning should preserve an explicit challenge surface:
- strongest evidence against the recommendation;
- uncertainty that could matter;
- conditions under which the recommendation becomes wrong;
- a credible alternative when one exists.

The system should not manufacture disagreement when evidence is one-sided. The requirement is **evidence-backed challenge**, not contrarian theater.

## Conversation as learning material

Dense founder–AI collaboration is valuable learning material when it produces durable, attributable evidence such as:
- explicit owner directives;
- accepted/rejected/modified proposals;
- implementation choices and reversals;
- production incidents and recoveries;
- measured outcomes;
- repeated operating preferences;
- discovered friction that leads to a verified improvement.

But **conversation itself is not automatic model training and is not authority**.

The safe path is:

`conversation → candidate signal / explicit decision → verified implementation or observation → durable project/decision memory → later outcome → evidence-backed lesson`

Casual wording, brainstorming, public-user frequency, or retrospective explanation must not silently become Founder DNA or policy.

## Hindsight protection

When an outcome is already known and no pre-outcome decision record exists:
- label the reconstruction as retrospective;
- do not invent prior confidence or expected outcome;
- do not score it as a calibrated prediction;
- do not treat it as equally strong Founder DNA evidence;
- preserve it only as contextual historical operating knowledge unless corroborated by contemporaneous evidence.

## Relationship to current implementation

Already present in production:
- append-only owner Decision Ledger;
- accept / defer / reject / modify dispositions;
- required rationale;
- expected outcome field;
- exact Brain / Cognitive Stack binding;
- optional probability and review horizon;
- future outcome settlement from later coherent observations;
- confidence calibration with warming threshold;
- lessons only from settled outcomes;
- execution authority = none.

Current evidence-quality gaps identified on 2026-08-14:
- evaluation criterion is not yet a first-class Decision Ledger field;
- counterevidence / invalidation condition is not yet a first-class Decision Ledger field;
- decision-quality vs outcome-quality separation is not yet explicitly encoded in the learning policy.

These gaps should be closed only when doing so improves the next real decision workflow. They do **not** justify a separate Experience-Aware Brain engine or Founder DNA runtime today.

## Activation rule for future schema changes

Before the next material owner decision intended for calibration / Founder DNA, prefer recording the missing evidence explicitly. A minimal extension of the existing Decision Memory schema is justified if it can be done without duplicating stores or creating parallel orchestration.

Until then, this canon is the authoritative interpretation rule for future experience records.

## Operating conclusion

**The Holding learns densely by turning real work into pre-committed, evidence-bound decision → outcome records — not by remembering more words.**

The objective is not maximum memory volume. The objective is increasingly accurate judgment.
