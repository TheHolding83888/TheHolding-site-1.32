# 4. AUDIO Q6–Q10 — CANONICAL OWNER LOGIC

## Q6 — concentration, winner growth, locks, liquidity and flywheel

Owner position:

- productive assets can be strategic long-term holdings and may be locked for years;
- the primary objective is cash flow plus long-horizon capital development, not short-term exit optimization;
- a 2–3x move by itself does **not** require reducing a strong locked position;
- strong locked positions may simply continue producing cash flow while new rewards are redirected elsewhere;
- owner may extend/relock positions to preserve voting/economic power as lock strength decays;
- possible architecture: multiple locks with different maturities, plus a liquid portion where useful;
- ecosystem horizon is closer to 3–5+ years than 6–12 months;
- 2–4 year locks can therefore be rational if thesis/economic rights justify them;
- for liquid/unlocked assets, very large appreciation (examples: 5–10x or more) can justify partial laddered selling;
- those multiples are examples, **not automatic sell triggers**;
- broad preference is not to completely abandon strong assets solely because they appreciated;
- generated rewards/cash flow can strengthen BTC, ETH, gold, silver, productive assets, stable reserves, RWA or other opportunities;
- strategy metaphor: **flywheel / snowball** — stronger foundation → more resilience / optionality → productive cash flow → reinvestment → stronger balance sheet → future cash-flow potential;
- market cycles "breathe": during strong markets the company should extract/harvest value and strengthen balance-sheet buffers so later corrections are less destructive.

Important epistemic constraints:

- no hard concentration threshold was specified;
- no automatic 5x/10x sell rule exists;
- foundation → borrowing capacity depends on protocol/collateral conditions;
- flywheel is a strategic model, not a guaranteed outcome.

## Q7 — Health Factor / leverage as a regime model

Owner heuristic:

- general comfortable orientation: roughly **HF 1.8–2 or higher**;
- prolonged weak market + active monitoring: roughly **1.5–2 may be situationally acceptable**;
- strong/bull market: **2.5–3** can naturally occur / may be desirable;
- overheated market after long bull run: owner wants more buffer, roughly **2–2.5–3** contextually;
- monitoring frequency matters:
  - frequent/daily monitoring may tolerate lower buffer in some cases;
  - less frequent monitoring should require stronger buffer;
- collateral type, volatility, protocol mechanics, liquidation design and stable reserves all matter;
- numbers are contextual heuristics, not universal hard thresholds;
- Aave was described by owner as operationally more abrupt in liquidation behavior;
- Curve **LlamaLend / LLAMMA** was intended by voice transcription (not "LlamaLand"); owner sees softer liquidation dynamics as potentially useful, not liquidation-proof.

1. Owner Decision Context becomes a first-class **read-only Ask source**.
2. Router understands intents for:
   - owner-context;

   - Knowledge Graph Growth;

   - HF regime owner logic;

   - `owner-context`

5. Strong relevance guard before returning adjacent public sources.

   - reject universal causal claims without mechanism-specific evidence;
   - do not answer a causal question with merely correlated metrics.
7. Governance-action guard:
   - owner-context action requests must explicitly answer `execution authority = none`, not generic unknown.

9. No automatic promotion of candidate metrics/edges.
10. Re-run the same 150Q Graph Gap corpus afterward to measure improvement.

This is a real measured gap, not speculative architecture work.
