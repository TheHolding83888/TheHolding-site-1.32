## 1. Owner direction

The Holding should progressively learn to see the economic system around its companies, funds and protocols — not only balances, APRs and rewards in isolation, but the flows and relationships that produce them.

The long-run objective is to turn transparent onchain state into a growing longitudinal model of how capital, incentives, liquidity, activity and yield interact.

This is not a mandate to fabricate predictions. The system must first observe, preserve, compare and falsify patterns. Only after repeated prospective evidence may it emit a bounded forward-looking signal.

## 2. Canonical capability ladder

`OBSERVE → CONNECT → REMEMBER → EXPLAIN → RECOGNIZE PATTERN → FREEZE EXPECTATION → TEST LATER EVIDENCE → CALIBRATE CONFIDENCE → EMIT COLD SIGNAL`

A signal is not a recommendation, guarantee or execution instruction.

The system must distinguish at least:
- OBSERVED — directly measured fact;
- DERIVED — deterministic calculation from measured facts;
- ATTRIBUTED — mechanism/accounting identity proves a driver relationship;
- ASSOCIATED — co-movement/context exists but causation is not proven;
- PATTERN CANDIDATE — repeated retrospective association only;
- PROSPECTIVE SUPPORT — later evidence supports a previously frozen pattern;
- PROSPECTIVE COUNTEREVIDENCE — later evidence contradicts it;
- FORWARD SIGNAL — bounded probabilistic/cold signal earned only under a separate prospective methodology.

No layer may silently promote ASSOCIATED → ATTRIBUTED or PATTERN → PREDICTION.

## 7. Prospective Pattern / Signal Engine rules

The existing Observational Memory + Prospective Pattern Evaluation methodology is the starting governance boundary, not something to bypass.

Required rules:
1. Retrospective recurrence alone is insufficient.
2. A candidate pattern must be frozen before later evidence is evaluated.
3. Support and counterevidence are symmetric.
4. Misses must remain in history and lower confidence.
5. Selection bias and sampling cadence must remain explicit.
6. A pattern cannot automatically become a recommendation, policy, causal claim or execution instruction.
7. Confidence must be earned from later evidence, never from narrative conviction.
8. No autonomous capital execution follows from a signal.

## 8. Data cadence principle

Different signals have different native cadences. Do not force all of them into one clock.

Examples:
- onchain market prices: high-frequency/30-minute canonical Market Data cadence;
- weekly ve/gauge epochs: native weekly cycle, with intra-epoch observations where useful;
- incentive deposits: event-driven or bounded frequent observation;
- fees/volume/liquidity: protocol-appropriate cadence;
- settled owner outcomes: event-driven after real economic settlement.

A missing observation is not zero and an absence of a recorded event is not proof of no change.
