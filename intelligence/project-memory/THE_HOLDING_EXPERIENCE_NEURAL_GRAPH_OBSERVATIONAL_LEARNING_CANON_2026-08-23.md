## 2. TWO LEARNING LANES

The Holding should learn through two complementary lanes.

### Lane A — owner-decision experience

Manual owner input is required where only the owner can truthfully supply:
- what decision was actually made;
- why it was made;
- the owner-specific hypothesis, intent or allocation choice;
- later human review where required by the production review contract.

This lane learns the owner’s real decision process from pre-outcome decisions and later observed evidence.

### Lane B — autonomous observational experience

- company capital / composition time series;

- generated / realised income and cash-flow change;
- trading volume change;
- fee / protocol-revenue / fee-capture change;
- voting-incentive budget change;
- reward-unit change and reward-price effect;
- concentration change;
- asset prices and market state;
- protocol/security events;
- performance history and other exact canonical deltas.

The long-term synthesis goal is to connect autonomous world history with owner decision history so The Holding can compare a current situation with prior evidence-backed situations, owner choices and outcomes.

## 3. EPISTEMIC LADDER FOR AUTONOMOUS LEARNING

Autonomous world learning must not jump directly from two co-moving numbers to a lesson or causal claim.

Preferred progression:

`exact observation`
→ `deterministic derived change`
→ `repeated correlation / association candidate`
→ `candidate hypothesis`
→ `tested association`
→ `accumulated experience`

A true causal relation is a separate stronger class and is allowed only when mechanism-specific methodology/evidence actually supports it.

Useful semantic relation vocabulary includes:
- `observed-in`
- `changed-after`
- `co-moved-with`
- `correlated-with`
- `preceded`
- `followed-by`
- `generated-income-for`
- `exposed-to`
- `pattern-observed-across`
- `hypothesis-about`
- `tested-by`
- `supported-by`
- `contradicted-by`
- `decision-about`
- `settled-by`
- `lesson-derived-from`
- `applies-to-mechanism`
- `applies-to-company`

Do not use `caused-by` merely because A happened before B or because A and B repeatedly co-moved.

Every meaningful observation/pattern relation should preserve sufficient provenance to recover the canonical source, observation period and semantic meaning.

## 4. OWNER REAL-YIELD SEMANTIC CORRECTION

A critical owner interpretation was explicitly clarified on 2026-08-23.

For the high-quality mechanisms intentionally selected by The Holding, **a high or sharply rising yield is not, by itself, a negative/risk signal.**

Owner semantic principle:

**`verified real-yield source + yield ↑ = positive economic signal`**

Reason:
when the same productive capital base earns a higher verified rate because real economic activity rises — for example trading volume, fees, utilisation, transparent protocol payouts or other mechanism-specific real activity — generated income rises. Even if the elevated rate is temporary, the company earned more during that interval.

Therefore:
- verified real-yield increase → positive economic signal;
- temporary verified real-yield increase → still economically positive for that interval;
- unexplained/ambiguous yield increase → `unknown → investigate source`, not automatically negative;
- high APR/APY alone must not become a risk flag or allocation deterrent;
- allocation may still be constrained by concentration, liquidity, protocol risk, company structure, available capital or other evidence.

Boundary:
this is **not** a universal claim that every high APY is safe. Ponzi/hype emissions or opaque incentive systems are outside this owner heuristic unless their economics are independently verified.

The graph and future observational learner must understand **why** yield changed before assigning meaning.

## 5. NEURAL GRAPH ROLE IN WORLD LEARNING

Neural Graph vNext is the semantic/provenance substrate for observational experience, not an excuse to inflate edge count.

The graph should help connect longitudinal evidence along paths such as:

`protocol → mechanism → company exposure → economic activity → yield/cash-flow change → owner hypothesis/decision → outcome horizon`

and autonomously observed episodes such as:

`observation → change → mechanism → company → later change → repeated pattern → hypothesis → historical test`

Graph quality should be judged by:
- answerability / retrieval utility;
- context propagation;
- explanation and provenance;
- correct epistemic status;
- ability to compare historical episodes;
- safe linkage between world observations and owner experience.

Not by raw node or edge count.

Do not create a second competing graph. Extend the canonical graph only where the new relation is source-backed and useful.
