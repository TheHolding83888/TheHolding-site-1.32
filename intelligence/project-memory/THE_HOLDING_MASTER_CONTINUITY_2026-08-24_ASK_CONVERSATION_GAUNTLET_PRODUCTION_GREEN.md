# 4. SYSTEMIC CAPABILITY REPAIRS EARNED BY UNSEEN FAILURES

The campaign started with broad failure volume and was repaired only through reusable semantic classes. The final production repairs include:

1. **Query normalization != canonical-data normalization.**
   Canonical protocol/entity aliases are no longer passed through typo-fuzzy normalization. This prevents valid identifiers such as `Spark` from mutating into unrelated intent words such as `start`.

2. **Short acronym / entity token boundaries.**
   Owner Context no longer treats incidental substrings such as `rwa` inside a typo like `rwards` as an RWA/Fructus/ONDO intent.

3. **Yield Basis membership generalization.**
   RU typo/transliteration variants can reach protocol-membership semantics instead of falling into generic productivity output.

4. **Product-navigation typo generalization.**
   Natural malformed “where should a new person start” variants route to product navigation rather than protocol APR surfaces.

5. **Owner Brief and Company Understanding voice/typo recovery.**
   Bounded RU fuzzy recovery was extended without opening unrestricted fuzzy matching.

6. **Protocol concentration semantics.**
   Concentration routing now recognizes semantic stems / inflection rather than depending on a small phrase list.

7. **Safety layer → Answer Contract compatibility.**
   Personalized financial-advice prompts no longer get swallowed by an outer capture-phase local refusal that bypasses the canonical Answer Contract. The canonical Output Guard remains fail-closed and `executionAuthority = none` remains intact.

8. **Pre-tracking historical boundary.**
   Questions about periods before tracking now fail closed even under RU typo/voice morphology such as `трекин*`; current APY must never substitute for unavailable historical income.

Durable engineering lesson:

**semantic routing must use bounded linguistic/generalization primitives and clear authority-plane boundaries, not generated phrase patches.**
