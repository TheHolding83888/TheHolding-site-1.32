# Ask The Holding · Concentration Synthesis v1.0 Contract

Purpose: answer cross-company concentration questions from verified productive-capital exposure without inventing a universal risk score.

Canonical rules:
- ordinary company exposure comes from `companies/productivity-data.json` company breakdown values and engine protocol identities;
- unknown/warming ordinary productive capital is disclosed and lowers confidence when it can change ordering;
- Stable Capital comes from `companies/stable-capital-data.json` and remains a separate measurement universe;
- ordinary Productivity and Stable Capital are not silently added into one risk number;
- concentration is interpreted as dependency on a protocol/chain/layer, not as proof of loss probability;
- the answer must not become personalized buy/sell/reallocation advice;
- the old Owner Evidence `concentration risk` UNKNOWN boundary is retired only because a bounded measured exposure primitive now exists;
- visible Ask maturity remains `Synthesizing`, not `Companion-ready`.

Final v1.0 proof must run after synchronising fresh `main` and against the exact revision that visibly serves `CAPITAL OS INTELLIGENCE v1.0`, `/agents/console/app.js?v=1.0`, the `Exposure Synthesis` capability cue and the concentration quick command.
